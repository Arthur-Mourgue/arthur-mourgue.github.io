LePotager

A robot arm grabs a fruit, presses it to feel how ripe it is, then puts it in the right pile. We had 48 hours, an SO-101 and a piezoresistive sensor that was still experimental.

Cart docking

The robot had to find a food cart and dock to it on its own, with a 3D LiDAR and a CPU only. [What made it hard, what you tried first.]

It estimates the cart’s pose to under a centimetre, in under 45 ms. In testing it docked 18 times out of 18, and it shipped to production.

Also at Capra: moved GDPR anonymisation onto the camera itself (stream latency −86%), and deployed a ground segmentation model on a Jetson Orin Nano.
