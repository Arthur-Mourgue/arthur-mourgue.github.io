Cart docking

Perception for autonomous mobile robots: ground segmentation on a Jetson, anonymisation inside the camera, LiDAR docking to a cart.

In production. It estimates the cart's pose to under a centimetre, in under 45 ms on CPU, with 18 dockings out of 18 in testing.
