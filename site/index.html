Sheet 01 · building

LaMain

An open-source three-finger hand for the SO-101 arm, designed with Julien Navet. It must stay under 200 grams so the arm can still lift objects, and it carries touch sensors on the fingertips.

The goal is to train robot learning models with touch, starting with imitation learning and then vision-language-action models.

Sheet 02 · on real robots

Capra Robotics

Six months on autonomous mobile robots in Aarhus. I trained a segmentation model that lets the robot see obstacles its LiDAR misses, and built a dataset of 1,840 images with ten times less manual labelling.

I also moved face and number plate anonymisation inside the camera, and built the localisation that lets the robot dock to a food cart, 18 times out of 18 in tests.

Sheet 03 · Most Creative Project

LePotager

At a LeRobot hackathon in Copenhagen, we built in 48 hours a robot arm that sorts fruit by pressing it. Colour can lie about ripeness, touch does not.

The policy is an imitation learning model trained on camera images and a low-cost pressure sensor.

Sheet 04 · paper, ETTC 2025

DagoBERT

At Safran Data Systems, flight-test engineers can program an on-board computer, but only in C. DagoBERT lets them describe what they want in plain English.

A local language model turns the request into blocks they can check and edit, and the blocks become embedded C++.

master's research project, early stage

Local rules for robot swarms

In a swarm, simple local rules produce a collective behaviour. I am working on the reverse problem. Given the behaviour we want, can a learned world model help find the rules that produce it?

The literature review and the proposal are done, and the first experiments are starting.
