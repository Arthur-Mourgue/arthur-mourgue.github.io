LaMain

An open-source three-finger hand for the SO-101 arm, designed with Julien Navet. It stays under 200 grams so the arm can still lift objects, and it carries touch sensors on the fingertips.

The goal is to train robot learning models with touch, from imitation learning to vision-language-action models.

Six months on autonomous mobile robots in Denmark. I trained a segmentation model that lets the robot see obstacles its LiDAR misses, on a dataset built with ten times less manual labelling.

LePotager

In 48 hours at a LeRobot hackathon, we built a robot arm that sorts fruit by pressing it. Colour can lie about ripeness, touch does not.

The policy is an imitation learning model trained on camera images and a low-cost pressure sensor.

DagoBERT

At Safran, flight-test engineers can program an on-board computer, but only in C. DagoBERT lets them describe what they want in plain English.

A local language model turns the request into blocks they can check and edit, and the blocks become embedded C++.

MSc research project · early stage

Local rules for robot swarms

In a swarm, simple local rules produce a collective behaviour. I am working on the reverse problem. Given the behaviour we want, can a learned world model help find the rules that produce it?

The literature review and the proposal are done, and the first experiments are starting.
