research, early stage

Local rules for robot swarms

Research project for my master's degree at Sorbonne Université. In a swarm, simple local rules produce a collective behaviour. I am working on the reverse problem. Given the behaviour we want, can a learned world model help find the rules that produce it? The literature review and the proposal are done, and the first experiments are starting.

Earlier work

Vision for water quality monitoring, INSA Lyon. A computer vision pipeline for BioMAÉ that detects gammarids, small crustaceans used to monitor water quality. It combines a YOLOv11-N detector and a MobileNetV3 model and reaches about 90% accuracy. I co-authored a technical paper on it.

Buddy robot, Blue Frog Robotics, 2023. A two-month internship on Buddy, a social robot. I integrated a distance sensor with 64 zones into the robot's head, from the firmware on the STM32 microcontroller to an Android app that displays the measurements. I then wrote a simple algorithm that detects swipe gestures by following the centre of the hand over time.

RoboCup and the French Robotics Cup, 2022 and 2023. I kept two autonomous competition robots running through tournaments that lasted several days. I was in charge of mechanical maintenance and reliability, and I designed their chassis in Fusion 360. The team finished third in the RoboCup Middle Size League and first in the scientific challenge of the French Robotics Cup.

School robotics competition, Toulon, 2022. I won the competition of my university with an autonomous robot built around a PIC32 microcontroller and programmed in C.
